About This Project

This website is a student-made clone inspired by the original Pickaroo delivery platform, developed solely for educational and academic purposes. It is not affiliated with or officially connected to the original Pickaroo application.

The current prototype features partner stores including Jollibee and 7-Eleven, with future plans for additional stores, improved rider systems, online payments, and delivery tracking.
